1. What the app does

Pagewren turns the photos and notes you add about your child into the pages of a book, organised into chapters by month or by your child’s age, with milestones, monthly photos and PDF export. It is a keepsake, not a medical, health or child-development tool: milestones in the app are prompts for memories, not an assessment of how your child is developing. Talk to your pediatrician about any question about your child’s health or development.

3. Free features

Without a purchase you can create, edit and read unlimited photo and text memories; read your book with its contents, milestones and monthly photos; add photos in bulk; export your book as a PDF in the Classic theme; back up, restore and export all of your data; and preview the Pro themes. What you have saved is never locked behind a purchase.

4. Pagewren Pro

Pagewren Pro unlocks the Pro book themes (currently Linen and Scrapbook), custom paper and ink colours, PDF export in Pro themes, and Pro themes added in later versions.

Pro is sold in three forms with identical benefits, all billed through your Apple Account:

  • Lifetime — a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel; it unlocks Pro for as long as the app is available on your Apple Account.
  • Yearly — an auto-renewing subscription with a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. Cancel during the trial and you are not charged; if you do not cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, it converts to a paid yearly subscription. Trial eligibility is decided by the App Store.
  • Monthly — an auto-renewing subscription with no free trial.

A subscription renews automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and the renewal is charged within the 24 hours before the period ends. You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your Apple Account settings.

All three forms support Family Sharing: if you share purchases with your family through Apple, family members can use Pro on their own devices. Each person’s book stays on their own device.

Prices, trials, taxes and availability vary by region, and the price that applies to you is shown on the purchase screen before you confirm. After reinstalling or switching device, Settings › Pro › Restore Purchases reconnects an existing purchase to the same Apple Account.

If a subscription ends or a purchase is refunded, your book returns to the Classic theme with all of its memories and photos intact, and the app remembers your Pro theme in case you subscribe again. PDFs you already exported are yours to keep.
