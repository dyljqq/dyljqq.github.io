Pagewren Support

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Pagewren is a baby memory book for iPhone. If something isn’t working, or you have a question or an idea, email qinqiangji@gmail.com. It helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version and the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings).

Your book and your data

Where is my book stored?

On your iPhone. Pagewren has no account and no server, so your photos and words never leave your device unless you share or export them. If iCloud Backup is on, your book is included in your iPhone’s backup; if it is off, it exists only on this iPhone.

How do I move my book to a new iPhone?

Restoring your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup brings Pagewren’s data with it. You can also go to Settings › Your Data › Export My Data on the old iPhone, save the ZIP file to Files, iCloud Drive or AirDrop it across, then use Import Backup on the new iPhone.

Does my book sync between devices?

Not at the moment. Each iPhone keeps its own book. Use Export My Data and Import Backup to copy a book from one iPhone to another.

How do I delete my data?

Open a memory and choose Delete to remove it, or use Settings › Your Data › Delete Local Data to remove the whole book from this iPhone. Deleting the app also removes it. We hold no copy, so this cannot be undone — export a backup first if you might want it again.

Making the book

How do I add photos I already have?

Touch and hold the + button and choose Add from Photos, then pick as many photos as you like; Pagewren places each one on the date it was taken. You pick photos with the iOS photo picker, so the app never needs access to your whole library.

How do I export a PDF?

Tap the share button at the top of your book to open Export PDF. Choose which pages to include, tap Create PDF, then share or save the file. Free books export in the Classic theme; Pro books export in the theme you are using.

Pagewren Pro

How do I restore my purchase?

Sign in to the App Store with the Apple Account you bought Pro with, then go to Settings › Pro › Restore Purchases. If Pro was shared with you through Family Sharing, make sure purchase sharing is on for your family.

How do I cancel a subscription or free trial?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Pagewren. You can also open your App Store subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or the current period ends to avoid the next charge. Lifetime is a one-time purchase, so there is nothing to cancel.

What happens to my book if Pro ends?

Nothing is lost. Your book returns to the Classic theme with every memory and photo intact, and your Pro theme comes back if you subscribe again.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy and terms

Your book stays on your device. The app sends only anonymous usage analytics that never include your child’s name, birthday, photos or anything you write. Read the full Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
