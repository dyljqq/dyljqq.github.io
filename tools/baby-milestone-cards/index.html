Month photos that look good together

  • Same place, same time of day. A blanket on the floor by the same window makes twelve photos look like one set.
  • Daylight, no flash. Soft window light is kinder to a baby’s skin and eyes than the flash.
  • Card next to the baby, not on top. Shoot from above so the card and the baby are both flat and sharp.
  • One thing that stays. The same soft toy in every photo shows how fast they grow.

Print the whole set

Print all 13 monthly cards puts Hello world, 1 to 11 months and 1 year on your paper, four cards to a page. The printed set uses the name and background you picked; the photo stays only on the card you save.

Questions people ask

Are these baby milestone cards free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many cards you make or print.

Is my baby’s photo uploaded anywhere?

No. Your browser draws the card on this page, and the photo never leaves your device.

What size are the cards?

A saved card is 1080 × 1350 pixels (4:5), a shape that fits messages and Instagram posts. The printed set puts four cards on each page.

How do I save the printable cards as a PDF?

Click Print all 13 monthly cards and choose Save as PDF as the printer. On a Mac, open the PDF menu in the print dialog and choose Save as PDF.

Which milestones can I make?

Hello world for the birth, every month from 1 to 11, 1 year, and firsts: first smile, first tooth, first steps, first word, first solid food, slept through the night, first trip and first Christmas, or your own text.

Is there an app for a baby’s monthly photos?

Pagewren: Baby Memory Book is our iPhone app for it. Since version 1.1 each month gets one photo on its own chapter page, chapters go by age, and you can mark milestones like First Smile or add your own. The Classic theme and three page backgrounds are free, with no ads.

More questions

Where can I get free printable baby milestone cards as a PDF?

On this page: click Print all 13 monthly cards and save them as a PDF from the print dialog.

Can I make milestone cards with my own photo?

Yes. Add a photo and the card puts it in a frame above the milestone, name and date. Drag the Photo position slider to choose the part that shows.

What are good ideas for baby monthly photos?

Keep the place, the light and one toy the same every month, and lay the card next to the baby. The tips above go through each one.
