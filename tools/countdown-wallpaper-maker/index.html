Free online countdown wallpaper maker

Pick a date, add your own photo and download a countdown wallpaper for your iPhone Lock Screen or your computer. Free, no sign-up, no watermark. Your browser draws the picture on this page, so the photo never leaves your device.

Background

On iPhone, tap Save wallpaper and then Save Image, or touch and hold the picture and tap Save to Photos.

On a Mac, right-click the downloaded file in Finder and choose Set Desktop Picture. On Windows, right-click it and choose Set as desktop background.

A wallpaper won’t count down. It keeps the number it shows today. On iPhone, the free Countdown widget shows a number that changes by itself every midnight, on the Lock Screen or the Home Screen.

Get the Countdown widget, free

Set it as your iPhone Lock Screen

  1. Tap Save wallpaper, then Save Image. Or touch and hold the picture and tap Save to Photos.
  2. Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap +, tap Photos and choose the wallpaper.
  3. Pinch or drag to adjust it if you like, tap Add, then Set as Wallpaper Pair.

The Lock Screen clock and widgets sit at the top of the screen, so the maker places your number in the lower half. That is what the grey time in the preview is for.

Make the number count down by itself

A wallpaper keeps the number it was made with. A widget is the part of the Lock Screen that changes: in Countdown Widget: Any Event every widget size on the Lock Screen and the Home Screen is free, with unlimited countdowns and no ads.

  • Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, choose the Lock Screen, tap Add Widgets under the clock, choose Countdown and tap Done.
  • Home Screen: touch and hold an empty area, tap Edit → Add Widget (on iOS 17, tap +), search for Countdown, pick a size and tap Add Widget.

If you switch to the widget, use a photo without a number as your wallpaper, so the screen doesn’t show an old count next to the new one. Lock Screen widgets are drawn in one tint over the wallpaper; on the Home Screen, the widget itself can show your own photo, which is part of Countdown Pro (how it works).

On a computer

Choose Computer (16:9) as the size. On a Mac, right-click the downloaded file in Finder and choose Set Desktop Picture. On Windows, right-click it and choose Set as desktop background.

Questions people ask

Is the countdown wallpaper maker free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many wallpapers you make.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. Your browser draws the wallpaper on this page, and the photo never leaves your device. The share link carries only the title, the date and the background color, never the photo.

Will the number on the wallpaper count down by itself?

No. A wallpaper is a still picture, so it keeps the number it had on the day you made it. For a number that changes every day, use a countdown widget instead, with a plain photo as the wallpaper; in Countdown Widget: Any Event every widget size on the Lock Screen and the Home Screen is free.

How do I set it as my iPhone Lock Screen?

Save the wallpaper to Photos, then touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap +, tap Photos and choose it. Tap Add, then Set as Wallpaper Pair.

Which sizes can I download?

iPhone (1206 × 2622 pixels, the screen of a 6.3-inch iPhone), Large iPhone (1320 × 2868, the screen of a 6.9-inch iPhone) and a 16:9 computer size (2560 × 1440). Other iPhones have almost the same shape, and you can pinch and drag the picture when you set it.

Does it work on Android or Windows?

Yes. The download is an ordinary JPEG picture, so any phone or computer can use it as a wallpaper. Countdown, the app with the countdown widget, is for iPhone and iPad.

More questions

Is there a Christmas countdown wallpaper that updates live?

A picture can’t change by itself, so on iPhone the “live” part is a widget: use a Christmas photo as your Lock Screen wallpaper and put the Countdown widget over it; the number goes down every day. The Christmas countdown widget page has the steps.

Can I make a countdown wallpaper for my laptop?

Yes. Choose Computer (16:9) as the size. On a Mac, right-click the downloaded file in Finder and choose Set Desktop Picture; on Windows, right-click it and choose Set as desktop background.

Can I send the countdown to a friend?

Yes. Tap Share the countdown and send the link. It opens this page with the same title, date and background, counted from the day they open it, so they can make their own wallpaper. Photos are never part of the link.
