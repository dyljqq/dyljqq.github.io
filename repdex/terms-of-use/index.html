1. What the app does

Repdex records workouts — routines, sessions, sets, weights, reps and notes — and ships an exercise catalogue of 1,324 movements with animations and step-by-step instructions in ten languages. The catalogue is stored inside the app, so browsing it and logging a workout work without a network connection. The app keeps your log on your device; it has no account system and no server of ours behind it.
