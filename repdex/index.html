Repdex

A gym notebook with the whole exercise encyclopedia inside — look it up, log it, offline.

Download on the App Store

Repdex is a workout log for iPhone. It ships 1,324 exercises with animations, thumbnails and step-by-step instructions in ten languages, all stored inside the app, so looking up a movement and recording a set both work in a basement gym with no signal. There is no account and no sign-up, and the log stays on your device.

What it does

  • Log sets, reps and weight from the workout screen, with a rest timer that starts itself when you tick a set.
  • Filter the catalogue by body part, equipment or target muscle, or search all 1,324 exercises at once; each one has step-by-step instructions in 10 languages.
  • Build routines and follow them session after session; answer four questions on first launch and a starting split is already there.
  • See volume, duration, completed sets, an eight-week chart, personal records per exercise, and consecutive weeks rather than consecutive days.
  • Use a dark mode made for the gym floor: in the dark the readouts get larger and heavier, so they read from an arm’s length away.

No weight tracking, no body fat, no calories, no meal plans. It is a training log.

Free and Pro

Free, always: all 1,324 exercises, all 10 instruction languages and unlimited workout logging. Your history is never locked, subscription or not.

Repdex Pro unlocks unlimited routines, advanced stats, CSV data export and home-screen widgets. It is sold as a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription — the yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial for new subscribers — or as a one-time Lifetime purchase. The exact price is shown on the purchase screen before you confirm. Full terms are in the Terms of Use.

Support

Questions, bug reports and feature requests: qinqiangji@gmail.com. Emails are usually answered within two business days. Telling us your iOS version and what you were doing when something went wrong makes it much faster to fix.

How do I restore a purchase on a new phone?

Open the Profile tab, tap the Pro card, and choose Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to the Apple Account that made them.

How do I cancel a subscription or the free trial?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside the app: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or the current period ends. Cancelling never deletes anything you have logged.

Does it work without a network?

Yes. The catalogue, the instructions and your log are all on the device. The App Store connection is only needed to buy or restore Pro, and the optional usage and crash reporting can be switched off in Settings → Privacy.

How do I get my data out?

Repdex Pro exports your whole log as CSV from Settings. Everything else lives in the app’s own storage and is included in an encrypted device backup.
