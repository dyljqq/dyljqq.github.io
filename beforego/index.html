BeforeGo: AI Itinerary Planner

Screenshot a travel post, paste a link, or just type your plans, and BeforeGo files it into a day-by-day trip plan: the places worth seeing, the to-dos before you go, and each day the post lays out. Scan a ticket or booking confirmation and it lands on the plan too. Meanwhile the packing list writes itself from your destination and dates.

Download on the App Store
  • Free to download
  • iPhone & iPad
  • iOS 17 or later
  • No account needed

What it does

Drop it in, get a trip plan

Screenshot a travel post, paste a link or just type your plans, and BeforeGo files it into a day-by-day plan: the places worth seeing, the to-dos before you go, and each day the post lays out.

Bookings on the plan

Scan a ticket or booking confirmation and it lands on the plan. Bookings keep a price and currency, and include shows and events, transfers and rental cars.

A packing list that writes itself

Plugs, currency, entry rules and transit apps for where you are going, generated from your destination and dates, with reminders 7 days, 3 days, 24 hours and 3 hours before you leave.

Places to go, things to tick off

The places you save become a trip checklist — spots, food and experiences to check off as you go — and a day of stops sorts itself into the shortest walking route.

Split the trip's costs

Add expenses in the trip's currencies, choose who paid and split each bill across the people traveling. Photograph a receipt to fill an expense, and Settle up shows the shortest way to clear the balances.

After the trip

Photos, notes and voice memos kept by trip, a printable memories album, and forgotten items that carry over so the next packing list starts smarter.

On your Home Screen

Countdown and checklist widgets keep the trip in view without opening the app.

Pricing

BeforeGo is made for personal travel organization. It does not replace official visa, safety, health, airline, or government guidance; always verify important requirements with official sources before you travel.

Pricing and subscription terms

BeforeGo Premium (auto-renewing subscription) A subscription unlocks all premium features: • Monthly plan — billed every 1 month • Yearly plan — billed every 1 year The yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. The exact price is shown on the purchase screen before you confirm; a one-time lifetime unlock is also available.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the current period ends. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your App Store account settings.

What's new in version 1.1.9

Scanning your tickets now builds the whole route, and Edit Trip can change who's travelling.

  • Scan several tickets at once and every flight, train and bus lands on the route in order, each stop with its own days

Questions people ask

What does BeforeGo do with a screenshot of a travel post?

It files what it finds into a day-by-day trip plan: the places worth seeing, the to-dos before you go, and each day the post lays out. A pasted link or typed plans work the same way, and a scanned ticket or booking confirmation lands on the plan too.

Does it make a packing list?

Yes. The packing list writes itself from your destination and dates — plugs, currency, entry rules and transit apps for where you are going — grouped by category or by timeline, with packing reminders at 7 days, 3 days, 24 hours and 3 hours before departure.

Does it remember what I forgot last time?

Yes. Forgotten items carry over, so the next packing list starts smarter.

Does it need an account?

No. Free to start — no ads, no account required.

What languages does it support?

Ten: English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, 한국어, Français, Deutsch, Italiano, Português and ไทย.

What does BeforeGo Premium cost?

Premium is an auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly, and the yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. A one-time lifetime unlock is also available. The exact price is shown on the purchase screen before you confirm.

Can I rely on it for visa or entry requirements?

No. BeforeGo is made for personal travel organization and does not replace official visa, safety, health, airline or government guidance. Always verify important requirements with official sources before you travel.
