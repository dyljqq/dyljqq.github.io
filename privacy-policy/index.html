BeforeGo Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

BeforeGo helps you plan trips, manage checklists, and save travel memories. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Information We Use

BeforeGo may store trip details, destinations, dates, checklist items, notes, photos, voice memories, place information you choose to add, app preferences, purchase entitlement status, and basic app diagnostics or analytics.

If you grant microphone, camera, photo, or location access, BeforeGo uses that permission only for the feature you requested, such as recording a voice memory, adding a photo, or attaching a place to a memory.

How Information Is Used

We use information to provide trip planning, checklist, widget, Live Activity, memory, purchase, support, analytics, and app improvement features.

BeforeGo does not sell your personal information.

Storage And Sharing

Your trip content is stored on your device unless a system service you enable, such as iCloud backup or widgets, stores or displays it according to Apple settings.

BeforeGo may use Apple services for purchases and device features. VIP purchases are processed by Apple In-App Purchase. BeforeGo receives product and entitlement status, not your full payment card details.

BeforeGo may use Firebase or Apple services for analytics, crash diagnostics, product improvement, and purchase-related operation. These analytics services may collect app usage data and a device identifier used to measure product interaction and improve the app. BeforeGo does not use this data to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy commitments.

AI Features (Travel Lens And Voice Transcription)

Two optional features send content to an AI provider, and only when you ask them to. When you use Travel Lens, the photo you take or select is resized, compressed, and re-rendered on your device to remove metadata such as GPS location; the prepared photo and the instructions needed to analyze it are then sent to the AI provider to return a translation and explanation. When you ask for AI transcription of a voice memory, the recording you selected is sent to the same provider and the text it returns is written into your note. Recording a voice memory by itself sends nothing; only a transcription you request does.

BeforeGo uses one of two AI providers, chosen by which one your network can reach: Google's Gemini API through Firebase AI Logic, or — where that Google host is unreachable, which is the case on mainland-China networks — Alibaba Cloud's DashScope (Qwen) service. Google is used whenever it is available. Each provider processes the request under its own applicable terms and data-governance practices.

BeforeGo does not operate server storage for these uploads and does not retain the uploaded photo or recording on servers controlled by BeforeGo. If you choose to save a result, the prepared photo, the recording, and the result are stored on your device and may be included in your personal iCloud data when iCloud sync is enabled.

Both features are optional and ask for your consent before the first upload. To show approximate price conversions, BeforeGo also requests exchange rates from Frankfurter using currency codes only; photos, recordings, and personal content are not sent to that service. AI-generated results may be inaccurate, so verify important details such as allergens, schedules, prices, and legal notices against the original.

Place Search And Maps

When you search for a place — to attach a location to a trip note, a check-in, or a plan row — the text you type and an approximate area around your trip's destination are sent to a maps provider so it can return matching places. BeforeGo uses Apple Maps, or Google Maps Platform for destinations outside mainland China. Your trip content, notes, and photos are not sent with a place search; only the search text and the destination area used to rank results. Each provider processes the request under its own terms and privacy commitments. A place you choose is stored on your device with your trip.

If you grant location access, your device location may be used to offer nearby places and to show your position on a map. This is optional: place search works without it, and you can change the permission at any time in iOS Settings.

Permissions

You can change app permissions at any time in iOS Settings. Some features may stop working if a required permission is disabled.

Data Control

You can delete trips, notes, memories, and checklist content inside the app. You can also contact the app owner through the support channel listed on the App Store product page for privacy support.

Children

BeforeGo is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app changes. The latest version should be available from the app and from the public Privacy Policy URL listed in App Store Connect.

Contact

Use the support channel listed on the App Store product page for privacy support.
