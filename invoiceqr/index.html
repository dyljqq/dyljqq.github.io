The fastest way for freelancers and small businesses to send professional invoices, estimates and quotes — and get paid faster with built-in payment QR codes.

What it does

Invoices in minutes

Pick one of 100+ templates, add your client and items, and share a polished PDF. Start with an estimate or quote and turn it into an invoice with one tap.

Scan a paper invoice

Photograph a paper invoice, or pick a photo, and it becomes an editable draft. The page is read on your device; the optional AI review sends it to Google only when you ask.

Get paid with a scan

Every invoice can carry a payment QR code your clients scan to pay. See paid, unpaid, partial and overdue at a glance, and share a paid invoice as a receipt.

Know what is still owed

Questions people ask

Is there an invoice app for iPhone that works without creating an account?

Yes. Smart Invoice & Estimate Maker has no account and no sign-up, and it works offline. Invoices, clients and QR codes sync through your own iCloud if you want them on more than one device.

How many invoices can I create for free?

Three documents in total, not three a month — invoices, estimates and quotes all draw on the same allowance. A document counts when you create it, and only once: editing it or sending it again later is free. No account, no sign-up.

Can a client pay directly from the invoice?

Yes. Every invoice can carry a payment QR code the client scans to pay. You then record the payment in the app, and the invoice moves between unpaid, partial, paid and overdue.

Can I turn an estimate or quote into an invoice?

Yes, in one tap. Clients, line items and tax rates are saved once and reused, and you can add discounts, shipping, payment methods and reusable terms.

Does it make QR codes for anything other than payment?

Yes. It includes a QR studio with 12 types — link, Wi-Fi, contact card, email, phone, SMS, chat, location, calendar, text, payment and Pix — and 16 ready-made designs, four of which are free.

Can I recover an invoice I deleted by accident?

Yes. Recently Deleted keeps invoices for 30 days.

What does Invoice Maker Pro unlock?

Unlimited invoices and QR exports, 100+ invoice templates, 12+ QR styles and 16+ ready-made designs, the full brand kit (color, font and logo), custom QR colors, dots and frames, and no app line at the foot of exports.
