What's inside

Every widget, free

  • Four widgets: one countdown, the next few in a list, a spotlight, or a tile for each
  • Every widget size, on the Home Screen and the Lock Screen
  • Unlimited events — there is no cap, and there never will be
  • Count down to a date, or count up from one
  • Recurring events: yearly, monthly, weekly, or every few days
  • Reminders on the day, and as far ahead as you want
  • iCloud sync, with no account to make
  • Import from a calendar, a spreadsheet, or another countdown app — and export any time
  • No ads, ever

Your days, kept

Most countdown apps lose your events at the worst moment — an update, a reinstall, a phone that died. This one takes a snapshot of everything on this device every single day, and always before anything risky. Thirty days of history, one tap to restore, and nothing ever leaves your phone. Days that have already passed are never deleted either: they collect into a timeline you can look back through, with the longest wait of each year written next to it.

The moment itself

When a countdown reaches a milestone — a hundred days, a week, the morning of — the app opens on the number, full screen, and gives you a card worth sharing.

The back of every event

Tap an event and it turns over to a page of its own. Write a few lines, add photos, record a voice — the story of the day, kept with the day. It syncs with iCloud, goes into every backup, and shares as one picture.

Made to look like yours

Thirty-six ready-made events, from a birthday to a first day at work, each already set up with the right repeat and the right reminders. Two hundred drawn icons and the whole emoji keyboard. A hundred and forty full-screen backdrops — twenty-two of them photographs — eighty-eight in Pro.

Countdown Pro

Pro sells the finish, never the function. It unlocks the Pro backdrops, with a new drop every month. Whatever you unlock while subscribed stays yours for good — cancelling only stops the new ones arriving. Your countdowns, reminders and widgets work the same whether you subscribe or not.

Pricing and subscription terms

Countdown Pro is an auto-renewing subscription. Prices for the annual plan, which includes a 7-day free trial, and the monthly plan are shown in the app before purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends, and you can manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings at any time. A one-time Lifetime purchase is also available; it does not renew.

Questions people ask

Is there a countdown widget app for iPhone that is actually free?

Yes. In Countdown Widget: Any Event every widget size works on both the Home Screen and the Lock Screen without paying, and the number of events is unlimited. Reminders, repeats and count-up are free as well. The paid tier only unlocks backdrops.

How many countdowns can I create on the free tier?

There is no limit. Most countdown apps cap the free tier at three to five events; this one does not cap it at any tier.

Can it count up from a date that already passed?

Yes. Any event can count down to a date or count up from one, and repeats can be yearly, monthly, weekly or every few days.

What happens to my countdowns if the app updates badly or I reinstall?

The app takes a snapshot of everything on the device once a day, and always before anything risky. Thirty days of history are kept and any snapshot restores in one tap. iCloud sync is available and needs no account.

What does Countdown Widget Pro cost, and what does it unlock?

Pro unlocks 90 of the 122 backdrops, with a new drop every month. It is US$9.99 per year with a 7-day free trial, US$1.49 per month, or US$14.99 once for Lifetime. Countdowns, reminders and widgets behave identically with or without it.

Does it need an account or an internet connection?

No account and no sign-up. The app works offline; iCloud sync uses your own Apple Account when you turn it on.
